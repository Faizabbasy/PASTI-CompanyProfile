## 1. Product

**e-CORPORATE — Enterprise Digital Platform.**

e-CORPORATE is an enterprise digital platform that connects **people**, **processes** and **information** in one integrated environment.

Main goals:
- improve collaboration
- strengthen governance
- improve operational visibility
- reduce manual work
- support better-informed decisions
- provide a flexible and scalable digital foundation

### Confirmed positioning (use verbatim)

> e-CORPORATE is an enterprise digital platform designed to connect business processes, people, and information within one integrated environment.

> Built to support organizations in managing their operations more efficiently, e-CORPORATE provides a structured digital foundation that can be tailored to specific business requirements, organizational workflows, and operational needs.

> By bringing key processes into a connected digital environment, e-CORPORATE helps organizations improve collaboration, strengthen governance, increase operational visibility, and make better-informed decisions.

**Key values:** Connected · Integrated · Flexible · Efficient · Governed · Scalable

**Short positioning:**

> e-CORPORATE is an enterprise digital platform that connects people, processes, and information in one integrated environment — helping organizations work smarter, collaborate better, and operate with greater control.

## 3. Design guardrail

If coding convenience or legacy layout conflicts with the design direction, preserve the design direction. No generic SaaS template.

Avoid: giant rounded SaaS cards, glassmorphism, neon gradients, dashboard collage everywhere, fake KPIs, fake analytics, random 3D, excessive glow, stock enterprise imagery as the main visual, overuse of pills.

Direction: premium enterprise technology — clean, credible, precise, modern, controlled — light-first with deep navy structural sections.

## 5. Responsive, motion, accessibility, SEO

- **Responsive:** desktop full composition; tablet reduced complexity; mobile recompose (not scale); no horizontal overflow; desktop diagrams are not forced onto mobile.
- **Motion:** restrained — reveal, subtle connection flow, section transition, hover/focus response, light parallax only if useful. No bounce, elastic or particles. Reduced motion resolves to a premium static composition.
- **Accessibility:** one H1, coherent heading hierarchy, keyboard-accessible interactions, focus-visible, semantic links/buttons, meaningful alt text, accessible form labels/errors, prefers-reduced-motion, reasonable contrast.
- **SEO (editable):** title "e-CORPORATE — Enterprise Digital Platform | PASTI"; description "Enterprise digital platform connecting people, processes, and information in one integrated environment."
