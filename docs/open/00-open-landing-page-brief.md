## 1. Positioning Utama

**OPEN by PASTI — One Procurement Ecosystem Network.**

OPEN adalah **end-to-end procurement ecosystem**, bukan sekadar aplikasi e-Procurement.

OPEN mencakup:
- e-Procurement
- Vendor Management
- e-Auction / Bidding
- Contract Management
- Catalog Management
- Collaborative Procurement / P2P
- Integration / Governance / Auditability

Penting:
**e-Procurement adalah salah satu core solution di dalam OPEN, bukan definisi OPEN secara keseluruhan.**

Mental model:

> e-Procurement digitizes the procurement process.
> OPEN connects the entire procurement ecosystem.

### Positioning copy (homepage / platform description)

Baseline:

> OPEN connects procurement, sourcing, e-Auction, vendor management, contracts, catalog, workflows, monitoring, and enterprise integrations in one transparent, integrated, and audit-ready procurement ecosystem.

Versi pendek (kalau space terbatas):

> An end-to-end procurement ecosystem connecting procurement, vendors, e-Auction, contracts, catalog, workflows, and enterprise integrations.

Jangan gunakan lagi framing **"e-Procurement connects…"** untuk mendefinisikan OPEN secara keseluruhan.

## 6. Business Impact / Proof

Menjawab: *"Apa contoh outcome yang pernah tercapai?"*

Baseline proof point:
- **Up to 40%** — pada contoh integrasi e-Auction / e-Procurement
- **67%** — reduction in procurement approval lead time, pada case Bank Mandiri
- **35%** — improvement in process visibility, pada case Bank Mandiri
- **100% digital** — approval & contract, pada case Lintasarta

**PENTING:** angka-angka ini adalah **CASE-SPECIFIC CLAIMS**. Jangan diubah menjadi universal product guarantee OPEN. Setiap angka harus selalu tampil bersama konteks case-nya.

Case Studies dan Business Impact adalah **dua section terpisah**: jangan disembunyikan, jangan digabung.
